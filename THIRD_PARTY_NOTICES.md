## Dryad workbook

The public data archive is Padilla et al. (2023), [Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.pc866t1w7](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.pc866t1w7), version 10 October 2023. The supplied `Compiled_Data.xlsx` and original README are redistributed unchanged in `data/source/dryad` with SHA-256 provenance. Dryad datasets are available under CC0 under its [dataset terms](https://datadryad.org/terms). Source authorship and citation are retained. Derived changes: normalize the isotherm sheet into 540 paired observations, preserve negative values, compute descriptive counts and ranges, and replot the measurements. Other sheets are archived without silently correcting their labeling anomalies.

## Wang follow-up workbook

The separate follow-up workbook is Wang, Pengfei (2021), [Dryad DOI 10.5061/dryad.3xsj3txf4](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.3xsj3txf4), version 22 April 2021, available under CC0. The unchanged workbook and SHA-256 provenance are in `data/source/wang_2021`. Derived changes: extract 72 kinetic means and standard deviations with source-cell locations; preserve signed values and unknown replicate counts. Isotherms are archived without resolving their unit discrepancy. Associated article: Wang P, Zhi M, Cui G, Chu Z, Wang S (2021), *A comparative study on phosphate removal from water using Phragmites australis biochars loaded with different metal oxides*, Royal Society Open Science 8:201789, https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.201789.
